Troubleshooting checklist
  • Open the app through a local server (for example http://localhost:8000), not by double-clicking index.html.
  • Confirm model.json, metadata.json and weights.bin are in the folder set by MODEL_URL and METADATA_URL at the top of script.js.
  • Open the browser's developer tools, Network tab, reload, and look for red (404) requests.
  • Check your internet connection: the TensorFlow.js and Teachable Machine libraries load from a CDN.
